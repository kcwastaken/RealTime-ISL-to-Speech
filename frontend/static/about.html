Empowering Accessibility Through AI

Why We Built This

SignSpeak AI was created to bridge the communication gap between the Deaf and hard-of-hearing community and the rest of the world. Millions of people rely on sign language as their primary mode of communication, yet everyday interactions—from ordering food to attending hackathons—often present significant friction.

By leveraging cutting-edge web technologies like MediaPipe, LSTM neural networks, and Google Gemini, our goal is to democratize accessibility directly in the browser. No expensive hardware, no heavy installations—just real-time, bidirectional translation that empowers seamless human connection.
