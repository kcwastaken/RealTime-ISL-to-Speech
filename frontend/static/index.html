HOW SIGNSPEAK WORKS: THE TECHNOLOGY OF HUMAN CONNECTION

Four Stages of Natural Synthesis

From computer vision to spatial transformers and neural speech synthesis, translate signs and voice in 14ms.

STAGE ONE • KINEMATIC TRACKING

Spatial Gesture Perception

Standard webcams capture hand movements without intrusive sensory gloves or dedicated infrared setups. Our lightweight kinematic engine extracts 144 discrete coordinate landmarks per palm alongside subtle facial valence dynamics.

✓ Wrist-anchored origin shifting
✓ Bounding-box invariant scaling
STAGE TWO • CONTEXTUAL GRAMMAR

Contextual Grammar & Gloss Mapping

Sign language is not a word-for-word mimic of spoken language; it carries its own intricate Topic-Comment syntax, spatial directional verbs, and facial modifiers. Our state-of-the-art Gemini NLP engine reconstructs smooth, idiomatic speech.

Keywords: [Namaste, Doctor, Medicine] → "I urgently need to go see a doctor at the hospital."
