How a session runs

Three steps, nothing to configure.

1
Turn on your camera
Open the live translator and allow camera access. Sign naturally — there's no calibration step.
2
Watch it appear as words
Recognised signs land in the conversation as full sentences and are read aloud automatically.
3
Speak your reply
Use the microphone and the avatar signs the response back, so the conversation keeps moving.
